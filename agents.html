Legislative Agents

Organizations that registered a Legislative Agent with the Clerk of Council, A to Z, with the firm and agents for each, from registration filings in Legistar since 2018. Dates run from registration to termination. Under Cincinnati Municipal Code 112-5(e) a registration expires on the last business day of each odd-numbered year unless terminated earlier; "expired" marks a registration that lapsed that way, and "present" means one that has not expired or been terminated. A year shows representation open during that year.
