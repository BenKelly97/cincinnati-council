About this site

This is not an official City of Cincinnati website. The official record of Council legislation is Legistar, maintained by the Clerk of Council. Where this site and Legistar differ, Legistar is correct.

Source of the records

Every record comes from the public Legistar web API for Cincinnati (webapi.legistar.com). The site lists matters introduced on or after January 1, 2018. Each record links to its page in Legistar.

Update schedule

An automated job runs once a day. It reads new and recently changed matters, reads roll call votes from recent Council meetings, rebuilds the data files and publishes them. GitHub, which runs the job, can start it several hours after its set time. The time of the last update is shown at the top of the main page.

What is generated by AI

The short titles, one-line summaries, topic tags, action types and neighborhood tags are written by an AI model (Anthropic's Claude Haiku) from the Legistar title, type, body and sponsor. They are not reviewed one by one and can be wrong. Neighborhood tags in particular are inferred from the title text, not from a map. The official title, status, dates, sponsors and votes come from Legistar unchanged, apart from the grouping described below.

Statements and Legislative Agent registrations get rule-based titles instead of AI titles.

Votes

Votes are read from Legistar's roll call records for meetings of the full Council. The site's vote records start in June 2020. Earlier roll calls have not been read from Legistar, so records before then show no vote breakdown. For each matter the site shows the final passage vote (passed, adopted, failed and similar actions). Procedural votes such as referrals, emergency clauses and motions to suspend readings are not shown. Votes are not available for committee meetings on this site.

How values are grouped

  • Status: Legistar uses several spellings for the same outcome (for example "Approve and File" and "Approved and Filed"). The status filter groups these; the record keeps Legistar's text.
  • Neighborhoods: tags are matched to the City Planning Department's 2020 statistical neighborhood names. Tags outside that list, such as places outside the city, are dropped.
  • Topics: a few rarely used topic tags are merged into broader ones.
  • Sponsors: name variants (for example a middle name in some records) are matched to one name per member.

Known gaps

  • Matters introduced before 2018 are not on the site.
  • 2019 has far fewer records than other years. The cause has not been confirmed.
  • A small number of Legistar records have blank or non-standard file numbers. They are shown as Legistar has them.
  • The Meetings page lists items attached to a matter. Ceremonial and procedural agenda lines (roll call, prayer, minutes) are not listed.

Corrections

Each record has a "Suggest a title or description change" link. Suggestions are reviewed before anything on the site changes. For the official record, use Legistar or contact the Clerk of Council.
